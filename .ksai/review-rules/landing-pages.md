# Landing page rules

These pages are YAML files under `app/_landing_pages/`.

- **Parallel items.** Sibling bullets, tile descriptions, card descriptions or table cells mix verb forms or subjects. Rewrite the odd ones to match. Tiles lead with a verb. Not a violation: the presence or absence of bold lead-in labels, or a list of two items.
- **Navigation and index.** A new page is missing from the nav, the index or the `next_steps` it belongs in, or a product-order entry sets the wrong index link. Say it in prose. Not a violation: index titles and descriptions that fill in automatically from the page.
- **No-information asides.** Remove a sentence that announces what follows or sells ("with visuals and key differences", "Unlike the other guide, ..."). Do not add asides to rows that are self-explanatory.
- Do not comment on landing page layout or information architecture after a release freeze.
