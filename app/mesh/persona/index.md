Three roles at Kong Air use {{site.mesh_product_name}} for different jobs.
Each page shows what one role owns and which policies that role uses:

* [Developer](/mesh/persona/developer/): routes traffic to their services, makes them resilient, and observes them.
* [Operator](/mesh/persona/operator/): runs the control planes, the zone proxies, the gateway, and the observability stack.
* [Security architect](/mesh/persona/security/): controls workload identity, mTLS, authorization, and audit.

To follow the same story step by step, see the [{{site.mesh_product_name}} scenarios](/mesh/scenarios/).
