Delete the system account that the zone wizard created for `zone-1`:

1. In the {{site.konnect_short_name}} sidebar, click [**Organization**](https://cloud.konghq.com/organization/).
1. Click the **System Accounts** tab.
1. Click the system account created for `zone-1`.
1. From the **Actions** dropdown menu, select "Delete".
1. In the confirmation dialog, enter the name of the system account.
1. Click **Yes, remove**.
