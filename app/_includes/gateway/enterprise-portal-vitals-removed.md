As of {{site.base_gateway}} 3.5, the product component known as Kong Enterprise Portal is no longer included by default in the {{site.ee_product_name}} (previously known as Kong Enterprise) software package. Existing customers who have purchased Kong Enterprise Portal can continue to use it and be supported via a dedicated mechanism.

In addition, the product component known as Vitals is no longer included by default in {{site.ee_product_name}}.
Existing customers who have purchased Kong Vitals can continue to use it and be supported via a dedicated mechanism.
{{site.konnect_product_name}} users can take advantage of our [API Analytics](/observability/) offering, which provides a superset of Vitals functionality.

If you have purchased Kong Enterprise Portal or Vitals in the past and would like to continue to use it with this release or a future release of {{site.ee_product_name}}, contact [Kong Support](https://support.konghq.com) for more information.
<!--vale on-->

Enterprise Portal and Vitals are still supported products for customers who purchased them. Kong continues to release bug fixes and security fixes for both. Kong does not add new features or functionality to them. If your organization only deploys supported software, these components meet that requirement for as long as your support agreement covers them.

For the official statement, see [{{site.base_gateway}} 3.5 breaking changes](/gateway/breaking-changes/#dev-portal-and-vitals).
