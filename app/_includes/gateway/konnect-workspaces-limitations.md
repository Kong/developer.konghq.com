* **Supported control planes**: Workspaces are supported on hybrid and Dedicated Cloud control planes. They aren't supported on serverless control planes, {{site.kic_product_name}} control planes, or control planes that belong to a [control plane group](/gateway/control-plane-groups/). A control plane can't be both Workspace-enabled and a member of a control plane group.
* **{{site.ai_gateway}} 2.x**: [{{site.ai_gateway}} 2.x](/ai-gateway/) doesn't support Workspaces. AI plugins, such as AI Proxy and AI Proxy Advanced, work inside a Workspace when running on a standard {{site.base_gateway}} control plane.
* **Workspace analytics**: {{site.konnect_short_name}} doesn't report analytics per Workspace. Analytics for a control plane include traffic from every Workspace in it.
* **Route collisions**: Collision detection always uses an exact match on path, method, host, and SNI. The configurable "smart" collision strategies available for {{site.base_gateway}} aren't available for Workspaces.
* **Shared entities**: A plugin or Consumer configured in one Workspace applies only to that Workspace. {{site.konnect_short_name}} doesn't support sharing a plugin or Consumer configuration across Workspaces.
